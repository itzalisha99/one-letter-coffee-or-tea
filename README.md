Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Letter: Coffee or Tea](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-one-letter-coffee-or-tea-89877fa0).

## The entry

`main.py` writes exactly one letter to `/output/entry.txt`:

```
A
```

**A — coffee.** One byte, no newline, no explanation, no punctuation.

## Why coffee beats tea here

The judge picks the letter whose drink feels more vivid, funnier, or truer:

- **Vivid:** coffee is dark, hot, bitter, aromatic — the jolt that turns a morning on. Tea whispers where coffee shouts.
- **Funnier:** coffee carries the jokes — the caffeine, the "don't talk to me before my coffee" mornings. Tea has no joke like that.
- **Truer:** coffee is the world's default morning ritual, the committed, identity-driven answer.

Tea is the only other candidate and it loses on vividness and humour. Anything that is not A or B loses to any letter that chooses coffee or tea, so the choice is binary — and coffee is the stronger side.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` and validates it against the bounty gate (exactly 1 UTF-8 character, not only white space, A or B).
